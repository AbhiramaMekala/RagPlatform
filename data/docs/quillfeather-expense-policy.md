---
title: Quillfeather Labs - Expense and Spending Policy
---

## Approval limits

Who approves a purchase depends on its amount:
- Purchases under $500 only need your manager's approval in Harbor.
- Purchases from $500 to $5,000 need approval from your manager and from the Finance team.
- Purchases over $5,000 must be approved by the Chief Financial Officer, Priya Castellanos.
- Any new software subscription, at any price, must also be reviewed by the Security team before purchase.

## Meals and per diem

When you travel for work or take a client out, Quillfeather Labs covers meals up to these limits per person:
- Breakfast: $20
- Lunch: $25
- Dinner in New York City, San Francisco or Boston: $85
- Dinner in any other city: $60

Alcohol is reimbursed only at client dinners and only up to two drinks per person.

## Travel

All flights and hotels must be booked through TripDesk. Economy class is required for flights under 5 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights longer than 5 hours. Hotels should cost no more than $325 per night in New York City and $250 per night elsewhere.

Rideshare rides are reimbursed for work travel. Late-night rides home are also covered when you leave the office after 9:00 PM. Luxury ride types such as black cars are not reimbursed.

## Remote work and home office

Every employee gets a one-time home office stipend of $750 in their first 60 days. Employees also receive $40 per month toward home internet, paid automatically with payroll.

## Learning budget

Each employee has a learning budget of $1,500 per calendar year. It can be used for courses, books, certifications and conference tickets. Unused learning budget does not roll over to the next year.

## Submitting expenses

Submit every expense with its receipt in Harbor within 14 days of the purchase. Expenses submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed. Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll run, which happens on the 15th and the last business day of each month.
