## Who we are

Quillfeather Labs builds finance-automation software for mid-sized companies. The company was founded in March 2019 by Marisol Okafor and Dev Raghunathan in a shared workspace in Brooklyn, New York. Quillfeather Labs has 142 employees as of September 2026, spread across the New York office and remote team members in 9 US states.

## Products

Quillfeather Labs sells two products.

Ledgerlight is our main product. Ledgerlight is an invoice-reconciliation platform that matches incoming invoices against purchase orders and bank transactions. Ledgerlight serves about 1,900 paying customers.

Tally Studio is a reporting tool that turns Ledgerlight data into monthly close reports for finance teams. The older version, Tally Studio Classic, will be retired on January 31, 2027.

## Internal tools

Employees use a few internal tools with their own names:
- Harbor is the HR portal used for time-off requests, benefits enrollment and payslips.
- TripDesk is the travel booking tool. All flights and hotels must be booked through TripDesk.
- Beacon is the paging and on-call tool used by engineering.
- Tollbooth is the internal payments gateway that connects Ledgerlight to customer banks.
- Orbit is the name of the release train that ships Ledgerlight updates every two weeks.

## Company values

Quillfeather Labs has three values, known internally as "the three Ts":
1. Tidy books - we treat customer data with the care of an accountant closing the year.
2. Talk early - raise problems the day you notice them, not the day they explode.
3. Take the stairs - prefer steady, compounding progress over shortcuts.
