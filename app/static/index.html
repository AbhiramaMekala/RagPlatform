    How an answer is made

    The sample documents are the private handbook of Quillfeather Labs, a made-up company, so no public chatbot knows these answers.

    1. Your question is checked for prompt injection and personal data.
    2. Two searches run side by side: one by meaning, one by exact words. Their results are merged.
    3. A reranking model rereads the best passages and keeps the most relevant four.
    4. The answer is written from those passages only, with numbered citations, then fact-checked sentence by sentence.
