---
title: Quillfeather Labs - Time Off and Leave Policy
---

## Paid time off

Full-time employees at Quillfeather Labs receive 22 days of paid time off (PTO) per year, plus 11 company holidays. PTO is added gradually each pay period, so a new employee earns about 0.92 days per pay period.

## Quiet Weeks

Twice a year the whole company closes for a "Quiet Week". Quiet Weeks do not use your PTO. The summer Quiet Week is the week of July 4. The winter Quiet Week runs from December 24 to January 1. Only the on-call engineer works during Quiet Weeks, and they receive two extra PTO days as compensation.

## Requesting time off

Request all time off in Harbor. For absences of 3 or more consecutive days, submit the request at least 2 weeks in advance. Shorter absences can be requested with 2 business days of notice. Your manager must approve requests within 3 business days.

## Carrying over PTO

You can carry over up to 5 unused PTO days into the next year. Carried-over days expire on March 31 if they are not used. Any unused PTO above 5 days is lost at the end of the year.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees get 10 paid sick days per year. You do not need a doctor's note for absences of 3 days or fewer. Tell your manager and log the sick day in Harbor as soon as possible.

## Parental leave

Quillfeather Labs offers 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave to all new parents, including birth, adoptive and foster parents. Parental leave can be taken all at once or split into two blocks within the first 12 months. When you return, you can work a 4-day week for the first 4 weeks at full pay.

## Sabbatical

After 5 years at Quillfeather Labs, employees can take a 4-week paid sabbatical. The sabbatical must be taken within 18 months of your 5-year anniversary.
