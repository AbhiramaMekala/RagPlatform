## Project Kestrel

Project Kestrel is the internal codename for an AI invoice-matching feature in Ledgerlight. Kestrel reads invoices, including scanned PDFs, and matches them to purchase orders automatically. The goal is for Kestrel to auto-match at least 92% of invoices without human review.

Kestrel is currently in a closed beta with 40 customers. The public launch of Project Kestrel is planned for November 18, 2026. It will be included in the Growth and Enterprise plans at no extra cost. Kestrel is led by Adaeze Mbeki (product) and Sun-hee Park (engineering).

## Ledgerlight 3.2

Ledgerlight 3.2 is the next major version. It adds multi-currency reconciliation for 14 currencies and a new approvals dashboard. The code freeze for Ledgerlight 3.2 starts on October 23, 2026, and the release is planned for November 4, 2026.

## Tally Studio Classic retirement

Tally Studio Classic will be retired on January 31, 2027. Customers still on Tally Studio Classic will be moved to the new Tally Studio automatically in December 2026. Custom report templates will be migrated by the Customer Success team.

## Pricing

Ledgerlight has three pricing plans:
- Starter: $49 per month, for up to 500 invoices per month and 3 users.
- Growth: $199 per month, for up to 5,000 invoices per month and 15 users. Includes Project Kestrel after launch.
- Enterprise: custom pricing, unlimited invoices, single sign-on and a dedicated account manager.

Annual plans receive a 15% discount. Nonprofits receive an extra 25% discount on Starter and Growth plans.

## Goals for Q4 2026

1. Launch Project Kestrel to all Growth and Enterprise customers.
2. Reach 2,100 paying Ledgerlight customers by December 31, 2026.
3. Keep monthly customer churn below 1.5%.
